# Incident Escalation Matrix

| Severity | Initial Owner      | Escalation                                    | Executive Notification |
| -------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| P4       | Security Analyst   | Security Team                                 | Normally not required  |
| P3       | Security Analyst   | Incident Commander                            | As required            |
| P2       | Incident Commander | CISO + IT Operations                          | Required               |
| P1       | Incident Commander | CISO + Executive Management + Legal / Privacy | Immediate              |

## Immediate Escalation Triggers

Regardless of initial severity, immediate escalation should be considered when:

* Sensitive personal data may be exposed.
* Privileged credentials are compromised.
* Ransomware is suspected.
* Critical infrastructure is affected.
* Business-critical services are unavailable.
* Significant financial impact is expected.
* Regulatory notification may be required.
* Executive or VIP accounts are compromised.
* A material third-party security incident occurs.
* Law-enforcement involvement may be required.

## Escalation Principles

Escalation should be:

* Timely
* Risk-based
* Documented
* Proportionate
* Consistent with the RACI model

All significant escalation decisions must be recorded in the incident record.
