# Post-Incident Review

## Purpose

The Post-Incident Review evaluates the effectiveness of incident detection, response, communication, containment, eradication and recovery.

## Review Questions

### Detection

* How was the incident detected?
* How long did detection take?
* Were existing monitoring controls effective?

### Response

* Was the incident classified correctly?
* Was escalation performed on time?
* Were responsibilities clear?
* Was the RACI model followed?

### Containment

* Was the threat contained quickly?
* Were affected systems isolated?
* Were credentials secured?

### Eradication

* Was the root cause identified?
* Were vulnerabilities remediated?
* Were persistence mechanisms removed?

### Recovery

* Were systems restored safely?
* Was business functionality validated?
* Did the incident recur?

### Governance

* Were required stakeholders notified?
* Were regulatory considerations assessed?
* Was evidence preserved?
* Was documentation complete?

## Lessons Learned

The review should identify:

* Control weaknesses
* Process weaknesses
* Technology gaps
* Training gaps
* Communication gaps
* Monitoring improvements
* Policy improvements

Corrective actions should be tracked to completion.
