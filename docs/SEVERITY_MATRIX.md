# Incident Severity Matrix

## Classification Model

| Severity | Priority | Response SLA   | Typical Impact                                 |
| -------- | -------- | -------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Critical | P1       | Immediate      | Severe business, security or regulatory impact |
| High     | P2       | Within 1 hour  | Significant business or security impact        |
| Medium   | P3       | Within 8 hours | Moderate impact requiring coordinated response |
| Low      | P4       | Best effort    | Limited impact                                 |

## Critical / P1

Examples:

* Ransomware affecting critical systems
* Confirmed major data breach
* Privileged account compromise with active exploitation
* Major business service outage caused by cyber attack
* Significant regulatory exposure

Required response:

* Immediate Incident Commander activation
* CISO notification
* Executive escalation
* Legal / Privacy assessment
* Continuous response until stabilized

## High / P2

Examples:

* Significant malware infection
* Confirmed account compromise
* Major phishing campaign
* Security incident affecting multiple systems
* Significant unauthorized access

Required response:

* Incident Commander involvement
* Security and IT coordination
* CISO notification
* Legal / Privacy assessment when applicable

## Medium / P3

Examples:

* Limited malware infection
* Suspicious authentication activity
* Isolated phishing event
* Limited unauthorized access

Required response:

* Security team investigation
* Documented containment
* Escalation if risk increases

## Low / P4

Examples:

* Low-risk security alerts
* Blocked phishing attempt
* Minor policy violation
* Non-impacting suspicious activity

Required response:

* Security review
* Documentation
* Monitoring

## Classification Factors

Severity should consider:

* Business impact
* Confidentiality impact
* Integrity impact
* Availability impact
* Data exposure
* Regulatory impact
* Financial impact
* Number of affected users
* Privileged account involvement
* Public exposure
